# Cyberpunk Oregon Trail - Game Design Document

## Game Concept
A terminal-style text-based survival game combining Oregon Trail resource management mechanics with a cyberpunk dystopian narrative. Players guide a group of hackers and data smugglers through the digital wasteland from the ruins of Old Seattle to the neon metropolis of Neo-Tokyo.

## Core Game Mechanics

### Resource Management
- **Credits**: Digital currency for trading and purchases
- **Data Chips**: Valuable information currency
- **Energy Cells**: Power for equipment and travel
- **Team Members**: Hackers, runners, and specialists with unique skills
- **Equipment**: Cyberdecks, jammers, and survival gear

### Travel System
- Distance measured in network nodes and physical kilometers
- Travel speed affected by team health, equipment, and route safety
- Random events during transit (cyber attacks, encounters, discoveries)

### Decision Making
- Branching narrative choices affecting team morale and resources
- Risk/reward scenarios (safe route vs. valuable shortcuts)
- Moral dilemmas typical of cyberpunk themes (corporate vs. individual)

### Health and Status
- Team member conditions: Healthy, Injured, Infected, Critical
- Equipment degradation and maintenance
- Cyberpsychosis and mental health effects

## Game Flow

### Journey Structure
1. **Starting Point**: Abandoned server farm outside Seattle
2. **Waypoints**: Various digital and physical locations
3. **Final Destination**: Neo-Tokyo data haven
4. **Journey Length**: 20-30 decision points with multiple outcomes

### Event System
- Random encounters with corporate security, rival hackers, AI entities
- Environmental hazards (electromagnetic storms, data corruption zones)
- Trading posts and safe havens
- Story-driven narrative events

### Win/Lose Conditions
- **Victory**: Reach Neo-Tokyo with at least one team member alive
- **Defeat**: All team members die or critical mission failure
- **Partial Victory**: Reach destination but lose valuable data/resources

## Cyberpunk Theming

### Setting Elements
- Post-corporate war landscape
- AI-controlled territories
- Underground hacker networks
- Corporate enclaves and no-go zones
- Digital ghosts and corrupted data streams

### Narrative Tone
- Gritty survival in a high-tech, low-life world
- Ethical ambiguity and difficult choices
- Man vs. machine vs. corporate power
- Hope and humanity in a digital dystopia

### Terminal Aesthetic
- Monochrome green on black display
- Retro computer terminal interface
- ASCII-inspired text formatting
- Glitch and corruption effects
- Authentic 80s computing nostalgia

## Technical Implementation
- Pure HTML/CSS/JavaScript
- Terminal-style CSS effects
- Local storage for save functionality
- Responsive design for various screen sizes
- No external dependencies for maximum compatibility
